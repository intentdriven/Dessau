Facts

  • net

    No port is opened to the internet by Dessau.Dessau cannot see your router; a port forwarded there would not show here.

  • out

    Every 24 hours Dessau asks huggingface.co about the 4 models you downloaded.It sends their names and nothing else, with no token.

  • rec

    Request statistics are recorded on this Mac: counts and timings only.Never a prompt, an answer, a key or a client address. Anyone who can open this panel can read them.

  • log

    Dessau's log is sparse. Debug logging is armed on no model.Model servers run at their INFO level and write no prompts.

  • api

    A program with the key, or a paired client, may unload a model it has finished with.Not a pinned model, nor one answering or inside its grace.

  • idle

    Self-test runs after 30 minutes of quiet; the context probe is off.Both stand down the moment anyone sends a request.
