# Taste: what the maintainer has liked and disliked in this repository's designs

Each line describes a judgement the maintainer made on real designs, with the
design history it comes from. It describes what was liked or disliked; it does
not override a new brief. A brief for new designs reads this file and says
which lessons it takes up.

## Keep

- **Large, comfortable text.** Body text of at least 17 px, larger for figures.
  The first round was rejected as "too small"; every second-round design, built
  to this size, ranked above every first-round one.
  ([control panel, 2026-10](../../work/design/2026-10-10-control-panel/README.md))
- **A polished, Mac-like finish.** The most polished design won ("8 is the most
  polished overall") even where it was weaker on finding things.
  ([control panel, 2026-10](../../work/design/2026-10-10-control-panel/README.md))
- **Detail in a sheet on the right.** Choosing something opens its detail in a
  panel sliding in from the right, with the page still visible behind it.
  ([control panel, 2026-10](../../work/design/2026-10-10-control-panel/README.md), design 9)
- **Clear, labelled bar charts.** One bar per day, weekends paler, today the
  darkest, a labelled scale and a one-line caption saying what it shows.
  ([control panel, 2026-10](../../work/design/2026-10-10-control-panel/README.md), design 9)
- **A "More" button that pops out a short menu** on a card, so a card shows one
  main action and keeps the rest a click away.
  ([control panel, 2026-10](../../work/design/2026-10-10-control-panel/README.md), design 7)
- **Simplicity that still works on a phone.** "Overall simplicity; works on
  phone well."
  ([control panel, 2026-10](../../work/design/2026-10-10-control-panel/README.md), design 6)

## Avoid

- **Small, dense layouts that make you work out where things are.** "Confusing,
  too small, and not intuitive enough."
  ([control panel, 2026-10](../../work/design/2026-10-10-control-panel/README.md), design 5)
- **Navigation that spends space on labels.** Group labels such as "Models" and
  "Sharing" above the tabs "waste space"; icons are preferred.
- **Buttons that don't feel native.** Buttons should look and sit as a Mac's
  own do.
- **Abrupt changes.** A card that expands should animate smoothly, "not
  abrupt", while respecting a reduced-motion setting.
- **A fixed bar that blends into the content.** A save bar that floats over the
  cards must be clearly distinct from them.
- **Cards that leave large screens empty.** On wide screens, cards should use
  more of the width.
- **A sidebar that cannot be hidden.**
- **Navigation that breaks at phone width.** "Menu bar breaks on phone."
  ([control panel, 2026-10](../../work/design/2026-10-10-control-panel/README.md), design 7)
- **Editorial and poster-like directions.** The calm editorial and Bauhaus-card
  designs lost every pair they were in and were the only two tagged "no" at
  first sight.
  ([control panel, 2026-10](../../work/design/2026-10-10-control-panel/README.md), designs 3 and 4)
